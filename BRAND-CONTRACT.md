# Love Finds Joy public brand contract

## Approved public identity

- Brand: Love Finds Joy
- Hosts/coaches: Mike and Alexis Hill
- Central belief: Love is great, but it is not enough. Couples have to choose joy.
- Joy means the relationship two people consciously build. It does not mean happiness, positivity or temporary satisfaction.
- Audience: Couples who want to grow together, not merely stay together.
- Founding offer: Choose Joy, a six-week founding couples experience.

## Public language to preserve

- Love is great. But it isn't enough.
- Love is a feeling. Joy is something you build.
- For couples who want to grow together, not merely stay together.
- The loop is the relationship you keep having inside the relationship you chose.

## Public language and aesthetics to reject

- Do not use Vow & Valor or The Hill Code as the active public brand.
- Do not use cream, beige, ivory, parchment or warm off-white.
- Do not use serif, script or decorative spiritual fonts. Use Arial/Helvetica.
- Photography in the current design studies is temporary only. Do not use any images from the wedding photographer in production.
- Production photography must be personally approved by Mike and Alexis before publication.
- Do not use AI imagery, generic stock couples, mystical geometry or synthetic diagrams.
- Do not present Mike and Alexis as therapists, gurus or a perfect couple.
- Do not imply that coaching can treat trauma, violence, coercive control, addiction or mental illness.
- Do not promise to save a relationship or tell couples whether to stay together.

## Human proof

Use real photography and specific lived experience. Their story is evidence of why the work matters, not proof that they possess every answer.
